The two problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. A day-first tenant’s 2 March comes out as 3 February. Every channel, every tenant on file.
  • hint: booking-v3’s form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field, but reads the date the tenant’s way. People type what the hint says and get the day and month swapped. Only the form channel, only booking-v3 tenants. The parser fix does not touch it: the hint must follow the tenant’s locale.
